Games 🎲

Three easy games — plus the AI prompt / vote finale.

♡ ♡ ♡

👶 Don't Say "Baby" — runs all lunch

Grab a clothespin at the door and pin it on. If you catch someone saying the word "baby," you take their clothespin. Whoever is holding the most clothespins at the end wins!

📏 Measure the Belly

Cut a length of string that you think matches the size of mom's growing belly. The closest guess wins a prize!

🔍 Find Someone Who…

Grab a bingo card, get up, and mingle — find a person who matches each square and collect their initials. First to a full row wins! See the card →

🎨 AI Prompt / Vote — the finale

Submit an AI-image prompt and ♥ your favorites. At the end, the top prompts get turned into pictures and played as a mini movie. Submit a prompt →
