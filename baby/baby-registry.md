# Baby Registry

Items others can buy without a wrong guess: common, standardized, and no personal choice required. Big safety and personal-fit items (car seat, stroller, crib/mattress, breast pump, rocker, dressers) are deliberately left off — buy those yourself or accept gift cards. Sizes are called out where they matter.
